          How it works

          Painting

          Pick a color at the bottom, then tap or drag across the tray. Tap a split cube again with the same colors in hand to turn it a quarter clockwise; keep tapping to go all the way round.

          The four grey buttons pick which corner the dark triangle sits in, for whichever split colors you hold.

          The cube you touch stays selected, and the four grey turn buttons turn it (or every selected split cube). With a keyboard, the letters and numbers on the buttons paint and turn the selection, and the arrow keys move it.

          Select picks cubes without painting them. Drag a box over them, or tap cubes one by one to add or remove them (hold Shift or Ctrl with a mouse). A color or a corner you pick then goes on every selected cube, and Copy and Paste repeat a block somewhere else, in this tray or another.

          Flip turns the whole tray over onto the table: every cube shows its opposite face, and left and right swap.

          Your designs

          Every design you change is kept in this browser as you go, and the button beside Help lists them all; tap one to open it again. Save there (or Ctrl S), or Share, keeps a design just as it is. Back them up to a file to keep them for good.

          Moving around

          Phone
          One finger paints. Two fingers move and pinch to zoom. Pick Move to drag with one finger.
          Mouse
          Scroll to zoom. Hold Space and drag, or drag with the middle button, to move.
          Trackpad
          Pinch to zoom. Shift and scroll to move sideways.

          Reading a code

          w r y b
          Solid white (cream), red, yellow, blue
          1 2 3 4
          Blue/yellow face. The number is where the blue corner sits: 1 top left, 2 top right, 3 bottom right, 4 bottom left.
          5 6 7 8
          Red/white face, with the red corner in the same four places.
          -
          Starts the next row. The rows and their length set the tray size.

          Short codes like 1M97I3… are the same tray squeezed for links and QR codes. Back to the main site for print files and history.
